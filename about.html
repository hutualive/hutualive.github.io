ABOUT KIBUFF

Intelligence
inside the device.

We bring the capabilities of large model to everyday device.

01 / our purpose

More capable AI.
More capable device.

We believe appliances can do more than follow fixed commands. They can listen, understand context, respond naturally, and take useful action.

Our purpose is to make that intelligence practical on everyday device anywhere.

02 / our technology

Small models.
Big capabilities.

Our device agent use small language model with fewer than 200 million parameters. Alongside them, we provide compact automatic speech recognition (ASR) and text-to-speech (TTS) model too so appliance can listen and talk.

We also provide unified multimodal model that bring listening, seeing, thinking, talking, and taking action together within the constraints of a everyday device for natural interaction.

explore our models →

03 / our approach

Think about experience.
Build for interaction.

01

Start with the device.

Memory, compute, and power are central to embedded products. We treat the target hardware as part of the model design, from the start.

02

Connect the whole interaction.

Listening, understanding, responding, and acting should work toward one purpose: making the appliance more useful to the person using it.

03

Train for the task.

We focus on the capabilities a product actually needs, shaping compact models around its interactions, data, and intended actions.
